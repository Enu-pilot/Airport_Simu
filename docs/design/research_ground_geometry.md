# Ground-maneuvering geometry: official manufacturer data

Scope: Boeing 737-800, 777-300, 777-300ER, Embraer E190, Airbus A380-800 (NOT FOUND, see Section 6).
All values are as printed in the source document. Values are ft / m as printed; where the source gives only one unit, the other is a conversion marked (conv.). "Nominal" = single value printed; no min/max exists in the sources unless stated.

## 5. Internal inconsistencies found in source documents (not corrected)

1. 737-800 turning table (4.2.5), 50 deg row: R1 = 18.2 ft / 9.5 m; ft and m disagree (18.2 ft = 5.55 m). Neighbouring rows (45 deg 39.5 ft; 55 deg 24.2 ft) suggest 18.2 ft is an error, not 9.5 m. Unverified.
2. 737-800 turning table (4.2.5), 60 deg row: R3 = 59.8 ft / 18.3 m (59.8 ft = 18.23 m). Small rounding-type mismatch.
3. 777-300 Rev E turning table (4.2.2), 35 deg row: R5 = 188 ft / 56.9 m (188 ft = 57.3 m).
4. 777-300 Rev E turning table (4.2.2), 70 deg (MAX) row: R4 = 132 ft / 43.0 m (132 ft = 40.2 m). The 43.0 m figure is consistent with the trend; the ft value may be wrong.
5. 777-300 Rev E clearance table (4.3), R4 = 152.5 ft / 46.7 m (152.5 ft = 46.5 m).
6. 777-300 Rev E general dimensions (2.2.2), engine forward stations: GE "84 ft 6 in (27.62 m)". 84 ft 6 in = 25.76 m (the Rev G 777-300ER drawing gives 84 ft 6 in = 25.76 m). PW "83 ft 10 in (25.66 m)" is 25.55 m by conversion. The 27.62 m and 25.66 m values should be verified before use.
7. All other ft/m pairs checked by script (tolerance 0.07 m for 0.1 ft values, 0.21 m for whole-ft values) matched.
